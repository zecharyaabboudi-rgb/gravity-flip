GRAVITY FLIP

Clear rows. Every 5 rows, gravity flips upside down.

How to play

  1. Fill a whole row with blocks to clear it.
  2. Every 5 cleared rows, gravity flips: the stack crashes to the ceiling and new blocks rise from the bottom.
  3. Watch the Flip in meter. It blinks when one more row will flip it.
  4. Blocks reach the edge they spawn from? Game over.
